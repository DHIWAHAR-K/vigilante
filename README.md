# Vigilante

**A private, local-first AI research workspace for macOS.**

Vigilante helps you investigate complex questions, work with supporting sources, and develop conclusions you can revisit and trust. It brings research conversations, evidence, notes, and AI-assisted reasoning into one focused desktop workspace.

## Research that stays grounded

Vigilante is designed for work that takes more than a quick answer. A research thread can develop over time while keeping its questions, source material, findings, and open uncertainties together.

The product direction centers on five capabilities:

- **Explore:** Break broad questions into focused lines of investigation.
- **Ground:** Connect findings to the sources and evidence behind them.
- **Remember:** Preserve useful context across a continuing research project.
- **Protect:** Keep sensitive research and AI workflows local whenever possible.
- **Control:** Make web access, model activity, stored context, and final conclusions visible to the user.

## Who Vigilante is for

Vigilante is for independent researchers, engineers, analysts, writers, and curious people who want AI assistance without giving up ownership of their work. It is intended for investigations where privacy, traceability, and continuity matter.

## Product principles

### Local first

Research data belongs to the person doing the research. Vigilante is designed to run its core workspace and AI workflows on the user's Mac, with external access used deliberately and transparently.

### Evidence before confidence

Useful research shows where information came from, distinguishes evidence from inference, and keeps uncertainty visible. Vigilante should make it easy to inspect the basis of an answer.

### User control

The user decides when Vigilante searches outside the device, what context it retains, which models it uses, and what becomes part of the durable research record.

### Durable work

Questions, findings, and source trails should remain useful after a single chat ends. Vigilante treats research as an evolving body of work rather than a disposable exchange.

### Focused experience

The desktop experience should feel calm and direct, helping the user move from a question to an evidence-backed understanding without unnecessary complexity.

## Current status

Vigilante is being rebuilt from first principles. Product definition is underway, and there is no downloadable rebuild yet. The first milestone will establish the smallest complete research workflow before broader capabilities are added.
